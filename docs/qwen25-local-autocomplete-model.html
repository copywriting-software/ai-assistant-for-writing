Why small models fit inline writing

Inline autocomplete only needs 3 to 15 words to finish a thought. A 1.5B quantized model responds quickly on Apple Silicon while handling both Russian and English text.

ollama pull qwen2.5:1.5b
cargo run -- complete "Привет, хотел уточнить по поводу"

Adjusting token and context limits

Lower max_completion_tokens to 24 or max_context_chars to 400 in config.local.toml if you want shorter, faster replies on older hardware.
