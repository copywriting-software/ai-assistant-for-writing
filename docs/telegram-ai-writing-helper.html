Fixing wrong-layout messages fast

When you type a Russian message on an English QWERTY layout, press Cmd + Shift + G. encoding.rs converts the characters deterministically before the proofreading model cleans up punctuation.

cargo run -- fix "ghbdtn rfr ltkf"

Word-by-word acceptance in chats

In short chat replies, you often only need the first few words of a suggestion. Press Option + Right to insert one grapheme-aware word at a time and keep editing.
